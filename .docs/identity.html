Brand guidelines · logo, favicon, home
virgule

The brand is the punctuation mark it is named after ("virgule" is French for comma). A comma marks a pause in the sentence: the moment when the group stops, listens to everyone, then decides. It closes the wordmark as if the sentence went on, because we often go for another round.

The logo

Bricolage Grotesque in extra bold, lowercase, with a comma drawn in papaya in place of the typographic sign. The symbol alone is used wherever the word does not fit.

On solid papaya: white comma
  • ConstructionA round head of radius 14 on a 64 grid, sitting on the baseline, and a tail that goes down to the left. No outline, a single solid fill.
  • Clear spaceLeave at least the height of the comma's head around the logo.
  • Minimum sizeWordmark: 72 px wide. Symbol alone: 16 px (favicon).
  • AvoidChanging the comma's color to olive or pomegranate: these two shades stay reserved for vote results.
