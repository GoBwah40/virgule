Virgule · recap on mobile

Seeing the scores in the recap

On a phone, there is no hover tooltip: today, the scores cannot be reached there. Three ways to show them, to try out directly (with a finger or a mouse). The examples follow your device's light or dark theme.

A · Swipe

Swipe the row

Your proposal: you swipe an idea to the left, and the score appears underneath.

The recap

← Swipe an idea to see its votes

Place
    • + A natural gesture on mobile, a very clean screen.
    • − Invisible until you know about it: it needs an instruction.
    • − Nothing equivalent with the keyboard or the mouse without adding something else.
    B · Tap · recommended

    Tap to expand

    Each row becomes a button: one tap expands the votes under the idea, a second tap collapses them.

    The recap
    Place
      • + The chevron shows there is something to see.
      • + Same gesture with a finger, a mouse and the keyboard; read by screen readers.
      • + Keeps the current spirit: details only when you ask for them.
      C · Show everything

      "Show the votes" switch

      A setting at the top of the recap shows, all at once, a for/against bar and the numbers under each idea.

      The recap
      Show the votes
      Place
        • + Ideal for discussing together: everyone sees everything at the same time.
        • + The bars compare at a glance between ideas.
        • − A busier screen once turned on.

        My recommendation

        • B (tap to expand) as the default behavior: discoverable, accessible, the same everywhere.
        • As a possible addition, the bar from C in the expanded details, to read the for/against ratio without counting.
        • A is pleasant but too hidden; I do not recommend it on its own.
