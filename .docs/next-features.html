Decide better, without giving up anonymity

Three features for the moments when “for / against” is not enough: saying how much you care about an idea, asking a question before voting, and reminding those who lag behind without pointing at anyone.

Per-topic option

Points voting

Today, Lea who loves the cottage and Tom who finds it “fine” weigh the same: one “for” each. With points, everyone gets 5 points per topic and spreads them as they like. The recap ranks ideas by total: you finally see enthusiasm, not just agreement.

  • Intensity counts. Put everything on one idea, or split between two good options.
  • Fewer ties. 5 points each separate ideas better than “for” votes adding up.
  • Nothing changes for other topics. The host picks topic by topic; “for / against” stays the default.

During the vote, everyone only sees their own points. Totals only show in the recap, as votes do today.

During the ideas

Anonymous comments

“Camping, fine, but are there showers?” Today this question goes to WhatsApp and the idea gets voted on without the answer. Now you ask it under the idea, in one line, and the group votes knowing the details.

  • No names, like ideas. Everyone sees “Your comment” on their own; nobody knows who wrote the rest.
  • Short by design. 140 characters, 3 per idea and per person: no endless debate.
  • The host stays in control. They can remove an out-of-place comment, after confirming. Authors remove their own.

Comments stay in the session: never on the room screen, nor in the shared recap, nor in exports.

For the host, during the ideas

Anonymous reminder

Sam sees that 3 people out of 6 haven't finished voting, but not who. No need to ask out loud “who hasn't voted?”. He taps “Remind the group”, and only those who still have ideas without their vote get a reminder.

  • Nobody is singled out. Neither Sam nor the group know who got the reminder.
  • No noise for those who are done. Their screen doesn't change.
  • No nagging. One reminder a minute at most.

Choices made, validated on October 6, 2026

  • Points

    A budget of 5 points per topic (or 3 or 10), several points allowed on one idea, an idea is kept from 1 point. Not combined with single-answer lists or the “for” vote limit.

  • Comments

    Anonymous, 140 characters, removable by their author and by the host. Shown in the session and its recap, never on the room screen or in exports.

  • Reminder

    Sent only to people who still have ideas without their vote, once a minute at most, with no count sent to the host.

  • Order

    Shipped one by one: reminder, then comments, then points (the largest, touching results and exports).
