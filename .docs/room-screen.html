Feature idea

Virgule on the big screen, a good idea?

Yes, for one specific use: a group gathered in the same room, everyone on their phone, with a shared screen (TV, projector, laptop on the table) showing the session live. Remotely, the shared screen is useless: everyone already sees everything on their phone.

In favor
  • A shared point of attention: we look at the same screen instead of each at our own.
  • The QR code stays on display: latecomers join without anyone sending them the link.
  • The recap becomes a shared moment: the decision shows up big for everyone.
  • Team meetings, workshops, family evenings in front of the TV: real use cases for Virgule.
Needs care
  • Anonymity on a screen everyone can see (see below).
  • Readable from 3 meters: little text, very large, no controls.
  • The screen must not go to sleep during the session.
  • One more feature to maintain: about one screen, three or four components, tests.
What I would not do
  • A separate "viewer" link: it would expose the ideas to anyone who gets it, with no seat and no name.
  • Showing scores during the ideas: forbidden by the app's anonymity rule.
  • Controls on the projected screen: the host keeps control from their phone.

The issue to settle first: "Your idea"

Today, the host's page marks their own ideas with a "Your idea" badge. If the projected screen simply mirrored the host's view, everyone would see who suggested what. The presentation screen must therefore be a separate view that only shows what the group is allowed to see: never an author, never a vote during the ideas.
