Virgule · proposal

Typed topics, step 2.

Three additions so the recap gives the answer the group is looking for straight away: the common slot of the periods, the compatible budget of the ranges, and two new topic types, place and list of choices. The mockups use the app's colors and copy; the examples are made up.

Period · in the recap

The common slot of the kept periods

Above the ideas of a "Period" topic, the recap adds a summary and a day timeline. Only kept periods count: they are the ones the group approved with its votes.

  • All periods overlap: the summary gives that slot, outlined on the timeline.
  • No slot common to all: we show the slot shared by the most kept periods, and how many.
  • Only one kept period or none: no summary, the list is enough.
  • We talk about periods, not people: the app doesn't know who is free, only which suggestions the group kept.

Range · in the recap

The budget compatible with every range

Same idea for a "Range" topic: each kept range becomes a bar on a shared scale, and the zone where they overlap is outlined.

  • Common zone: "Compatible budget: €300 to €400". If it narrows down to one amount, "Compatible budget: €350".
  • No zone common to all: the zone shared by the most kept ranges, with their count.
  • "Amount" topic (a single value): no zone; we can show the spread, "Between €250 and €480", if useful.

New type · Place

A place, with its link to the map

The participant types an address or a place name, as today. The app adds a "View on the map" link under the idea, which opens a search in the phone's maps app. No map is loaded in Virgule: no third-party service, no API key, nothing more to maintain.

  • Google Maps search link (maps/search/?api=1&query=…): it opens Apple Maps or Google Maps on mobile and the website elsewhere. OpenStreetMap is possible instead.
  • Duplicates are detected as for text.

New type · List

A choice from a list set by the host

When creating the topic, Camille enters the options ("Seaside", "Mountains", "City"). When the ideas start, each option becomes an idea ready to vote on: no input field, everyone votes on the same options. A setting allows other suggestions on top.

  • 2 to 10 options, no duplicates; editable until the ideas start.
  • The options belong to the host: nobody can remove them during voting.
  • The recap, rounds and tiebreak work as for other ideas.
