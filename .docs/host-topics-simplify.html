Why A rather than B

A · open on demandB · Simple / Advanced switch
First screenOne field, a few chips, StartSame in Simple
Need a Period topic onceOpen “Answer type and voting” for that topic onlySwitch the whole page to Advanced, everything comes back

CodeReuses ExpandableListItem-style folds, one layoutTwo layouts to test, a new preference cookie
  • Topic rows: edit stays visible; move up, move down and delete go into a “⋯” menu (44 px target, keyboard reachable).
  • Add a topic: a single field. Details, answer type and voting fold under one link; they unfold on their own when editing a topic that already uses them.
  • Topic ideas: three chips at most, and they disappear once there are three topics.
  • Participants' suggestions: merged into the list as highlighted rows rather than a separate card.
  • Settings: a one-line summary that unfolds; on wide screens it stays in the side column, folded too.
  • List / Board: kept for the host, but hidden until there are four topics (the board adds nothing for two).
  • Trade-off: one more tap for hosts who use typed topics on every topic. The fold remembers its last state per person to soften it.
