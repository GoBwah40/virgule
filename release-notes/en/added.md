# Added

New features, visible to the people using Virgule. Format and rules: [VERSIONS.md](../../VERSIONS.md).

## 0.6.0 — 2026-10-01

- Until the first recap, you can leave a session from the button at the top or from your own seat: your seat is freed for someone else.

## 0.5.0 — 2026-09-30

- Virgule is available in English, with a language picker in the footer; by default it follows the device language.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-30

- Release notes available from the home page.
- Tiebreak round when several kept ideas are tied for first.
- Optional timer during the ideas (3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes).
- The person hosting can hand over hosting to someone else or remove a person.
- App logo and icon.
- "Place" topics, with a link to see the spot on the map, and "List" topics, whose options are set by the person hosting.
- In the recap, the common slot of the suggested periods and the budget compatible with the ranges.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-09-30

- Typed topics: date, period, amount and range, with input suited to phones.
- Duplicate ideas within a topic are refused.
- Invite with a QR code and the phone's native sharing.
- Light or dark theme picker in the footer.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-29

- Loading screens and error pages with "Try again".

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-28

- First version: topics, ideas, anonymous for or against votes, and a recap per round.
- New round with only the kept ideas, reopening votes, and ending a session as read-only.
- Recap export in Markdown, CSV and PDF.
